Slayer

Pick a Slayer master and set up your task list: what to do, skip, block or extend, and the method for each task. The Goal Planner uses these settings for every Slayer step. Suggested lists are worked out for your account from the rates below.

Task rates, methods, weights and lengths come from KodakKid3's OSRS Slayer Spreadsheets (v3.8.2) — a huge amount of testing and research. See the original spreadsheet. Task requirements and the masters' points come from the OSRS Wiki.

Chance is how often the master gives the task, after blocks and the tasks you can't get yet. A skipped task costs 30 points and the time to get a new one. Tasks with no rate data are counted as skipped. Boss methods are always in the Method list; the suggestions only use them if that's ticked above. Expected points include streak bonuses; Turael skipping and Konar point boosting aren't modelled yet.
