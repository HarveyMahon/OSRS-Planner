My Account

Enter your stats by level or XP. A level is stored as the minimum XP for that level. Everything stays in this browser; other pages use it to filter and plan.

Quests

Tick the quests and miniquests you've completed. Ticking one also ticks the quests it requires.

Unlocks

Items that some methods need you to have earned.

Prayers

Tick the prayers you've unlocked (scrolls, Knight Waves, the Horn of Bran). The DPS calculator uses the best one your Prayer level allows.

Bank

Paste the export from the Bank Memory RuneLite plugin (right-click your bank in its panel, then Export). The planner uses it to see which gear you already own, so it only counts the gear you'd still have to buy when choosing a setup. It also ticks any unlocks above that it finds.
