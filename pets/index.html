Pet Hunt Profit Calculator

Loot by the pet drop rate

Average loot collected over the number of kills, raids, runs or actions that matches each pet's drop rate. Bosses with extra options use their default settings, and skilling pets use the top method on their page. Profit is that loot minus the supplies used, with losses in red, and profit per hour spreads it over the time to pet (per day for Tangleroot). Tick the pets you already own to grey them out or hide them. Time to pet and supply costs use Wiki money-making guide figures (hover for the loot, supplies and sources), or your own kills per hour and supply cost, marked *, if you've entered them in a breakdown. Select a row to see its full breakdown and change the settings.
