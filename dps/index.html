DPS calculator

Compare setups against a boss and tweak them slot by slot. Formulas from the OSRS Wiki's Damage per second pages. Levels, prayers and quests start from My Account; change them here to try "what if".

Type an item name in a slot (suggestions appear as you type). Leave it empty for nothing.

DPS uses the best attack style (and autocast spell) for the setup unless you pick one. Kill time is HP ÷ DPS with no downtime. Potions are assumed: super combat, ranging, and the heart chosen above. Pick a spec weapon to see the kill time with it: Defence-draining specs are used at the start, damage specs whenever there's energy (100% to start, then 10% every 30 seconds).
