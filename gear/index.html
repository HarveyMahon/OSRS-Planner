Best setup

The fastest-killing gear for a boss from what you own and what your cash stack can buy, using the DPS formulas from the OSRS Wiki. Gear you own comes from your bank import on My Account; prayers, quests and levels come from there too.

PartStyleAttackMax hitAccuracyDPSTime

Kill time is HP ÷ DPS with every attack landing on time: no walking, phases or downtime, so it's for comparing setups, not a real kill time. Potions are assumed (super combat, ranging, and your imbued or saturated heart if your bank has one, else a magic potion), with your best unlocked prayer. Special attacks: the Defence-drain and/or damage spec weapon that saves the most time, from one energy pool: a full bar at the start of the kill (or of the whole raid or wave run), then 10% every 30 seconds, used where it saves the most. Kill rates on Money Makers instead give each kill only the energy that regenerates over one kill cycle. A thrall is added (+0.625 DPS) when you've done A Kingdom Divided and it helps, which means the Arceuus spellbook.
